GRAFFITI GROOVE

FASHION PHOTOGRAPHY, 2023
Graffiti Groove is a fashion editorial photographed for Bliss Magazine and shot across locations in New York City. The project captures contemporary street style within the textures of the urban landscape, using graffiti, architecture, and public space as visual backdrops. I photographed the entire series, developing compositions that merge fashion photography with the energy and visual language of the city.
