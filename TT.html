TOUNGE-TIED

ANIMATION, 2024
Tongue Tied is a hand-drawn animation loop exploring sensuality, intimacy, and romantic tension through surreal visual metaphor. The short animated sequence exaggerates gesture and expression to examine the awkward, magnetic pull between two figures. Using fluid line work and looping motion, the animation transforms a fleeting romantic moment into a continuous visual rhythm of attraction and hesitation.

Size:

11 x 8.5 in

Roles/Skills:

Illustration, Animation

Testing initial color combinations for the characters
Organizing individual frames to construct motion across the loop
Tracing and refining hand-drawn sequences using a lightbox
