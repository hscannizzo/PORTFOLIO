BONES

ANIMATED SHORT FILM, 2024
Bones is a short animated comedy film inspired by a real off-roading trip in the Appalachian mountains. The story follows a duo whose night in the woods turns surreal after encountering a deer skull hanging from a tree. Combining humor with eerie imagery, the animation exaggerates the paranoia and absurdity of the moment.
