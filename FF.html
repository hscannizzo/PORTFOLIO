FOOD FIGHT

VIDEO GAME, 2024
Food Fight is a browser-based video game inspired by NYC Open Data on school health inspections. Designed as an “I Spy” style experience, the illustrated environment challenges players to locate hidden health violations within a chaotic cafeteria scene. I designed, illustrated, and coded the project, translating real municipal data into an interactive visual experience that highlights public health issues through playful exploration.
