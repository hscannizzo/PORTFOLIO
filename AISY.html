ALFAAZ

ZINE COLLECTIVE, 2023–2025
Alfaaz is a student-run zine collective I worked with from 2023–2025 as a writer, designer, and event organizer. During my time with the group, we produced four issues exploring art, culture, and student voices. I contributed editorial design and written work while also helping organize community events, including a zine swap and two live screenings of The Rocky Horror Picture Show.
