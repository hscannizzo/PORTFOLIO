THE YELLOW PAGES

BOOK/WEB DESIGN, 2023
The Yellow Pages is a research-driven design project documenting graffiti culture around New York City’s Union Square. I photographed and catalogued every sticker between 14th and 17th Streets, researching the artists, brands, and communities behind them. The resulting publication organizes identified stickers alphabetically while a companion website proposes a crowdsourced system for documenting anonymous street art.
