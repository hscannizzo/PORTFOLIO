CASAFILM FESTIVAL

PHOTOGRAPHIC SERIES, 2026
This photographic series documents an independent film festival and fundraiser held in a historic church on Manhattan’s Upper West Side. The project captures intimate moments of performance, music, and gathering throughout the evening, culminating in a series of independent film screenings. Through candid portraits and live performances, the photographs reveal the raw emotion and communal energy of a night brought together in support of preserving the space.
