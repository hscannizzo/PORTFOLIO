DUNE: EXPANDED

BOOK DESIGN, 2024
Dune Expanded is a hand-bound book that reimagines Frank Herbert’s Dune through an original typographic and editorial design system. The book includes the complete text of the first novel alongside an expanded guide to the series featuring character genealogies, planetary maps, organizational structures, and historical context. A miniature reproduction of the Dune Encyclopedia is included at the end, accompanied by a magnifying glass for reading.

Size:

8.5 x 11 in

Roles/Skills:

Book Design, Illustration

Analyzing reference material to establish color, tone, and typographic direction
Studying historical book structures and techniques at a Midtown archive
Assembling and binding the physical book cover by hand
